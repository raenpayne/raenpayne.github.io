This blog is written in English, and sometimes in Japanese about Japanese language and media. It's published via github pages and uses the Jekyll framework with the Chirpy theme. 

Am I an expert? No. I just love languages and Japanese in particular. I also really love kanji, which, I realize probably seems weird to most people who look at them and then process how many you need to learn to read a news paper or magazine or conduct daily life in Japan and, very rationally think, no effing way am I doing that. But I have some sort of mental health issue when it comes to kanji in particular and just can't help myself.
